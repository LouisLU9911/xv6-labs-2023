1. Without locks, the put and insert are not atomic, which will break the invariant of the hash table .
The possible event sequence which will lead to a key being missing is:
thread1: e->next = n => thread2: e->next = n => thread1: *p = e => thread2: *p = e
In the above order, a new entry added by thread1 will be overwritten by a new entry added by thread2.
